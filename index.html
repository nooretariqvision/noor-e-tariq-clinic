Clinical Excellence

Center of Excellence Specialties

Explore our 16 specialized vision care services. Use the filter search to find specific treatments.

Low Vision Rehabilitation Services

Comprehensive visual rehabilitation for individuals with significant vision impairment using non-optical & optical aids.

Myopia Management & Refraction

Evidence-based pediatric myopia control strategies and high-precision clinical refraction.

Visual Function Assessment

Detailed evaluation of contrast sensitivity, visual fields, and function in children & adults.

Binocular Vision Dysfunction (BVD)

Diagnostics and vision therapy for eye strain, double vision, and eye misalignment issues.

BVD in Autism & ADHD

Specialized sensory-friendly vision care and BVD management for neurodivergent children.

Dry Eye Evaluation & Care

Tear film analysis, ocular surface assessment, and therapeutic dry eye relief solutions.

Computer Vision Syndrome

Ergonomic advice and specialized screen lenses to combat digital eye fatigue.

Color Vision Screening

Standardized Ishihara and advanced hue testing for occupational and clinical needs.

Management of Squint & Amblyopia

Non-surgical patch therapies, occlusion lenses, and orthoptic training for lazy eye.

Prisms for Diplopia & Squint

Precision Fresnel and ground prism prescription to resolve troublesome double vision.

Management of Keratoconus

Specialized corneal evaluation and custom lens fittings for ectatic corneal disorders.

Specialty Contact Lenses

Expert fitting of Soft, RGP, Scleral, Hybrid, Impression, and Piggyback contact lenses.

Prosthetic Contact Lenses

Custom hand-painted aesthetic and functional prosthetic lenses for disfigured eyes.

Rx Glasses, Frames & Sunglasses

Wide array of premium prescription eyewear, optical frames, and polarized protective sunglasses.

Lens Kits & Care Solutions

Sterile multi-purpose contact lens solutions, travel kits, insertion tools, and accessories.

Low Vision Aids / Devices

Optical magnifiers, electronic video magnifiers, filters, and digital assistive vision devices.
